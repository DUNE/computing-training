# Logo sources

The cards show each project's own logo, so a reader can spot a familiar tool at a glance. The logos are trademarks of their projects and are used here only to link to their lessons. Most were taken from the [HSF Training Center](https://github.com/hsf-training/training-center/tree/main/src/images) (MIT licence), which uses them the same way.
